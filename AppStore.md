Train an army of brave hamsters and march through 5 ages of history!

EVOLVE YOUR ARMY
Start with clubs and slings in the Stone Age, then evolve to knights, musketeers, tanks and laser mechs. Each age unlocks new units, turrets and a devastating special attack.

EVERY BATTLE IS DIFFERENT
Each time you evolve, pick 1 of 3 Evolution Cards: Sharp Teeth, Vampire Bite, Splash Shot, Last Stand and more. Stack them into wild builds.

FAST & SATISFYING
Battles last just a few minutes. Spawn troops, build turrets, call down meteor showers and watch the rat base crumble.

COLLECT GENERALS
Unlock heroes like Sir Nibbles, Queen Squeak and Dr. Boom. Equip one before battle and level them up with duplicates.

ENDLESS CAMPAIGN
The Rat King attacks every 5 stages, and stage twists like Rat Swarm, Giants and Blitz keep you on your toes. Upgrade your base, weapons and training between battles.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
Survive endless waves in Survival mode, beat the Daily Challenge, finish daily quests for free crates, and harvest your idle Seed Farm.

Plays offline. No account needed. Available in 12 languages.

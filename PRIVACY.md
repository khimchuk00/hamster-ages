# Hamster Ages — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Hamster Ages ("the game") is developed by Valentyn Khimchuk. This policy explains what data the game handles.

## Data stored on your device
Your progress (stages, seeds, upgrades, generals, quests, settings) is saved only on your device. The game has no
accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer cannot see or access this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA,
only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt), approximate location derived from your IP address, device information
and ad interaction data to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.
- In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any
  time in **Settings → Privacy Choices**.
- If you decline tracking in the iOS "Allow Tracking" prompt, ads are not personalised with your IDFA.
- Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## In-app purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple. The game never sees your payment details; it only receives confirmation of what was
bought so it can unlock it.

## Game Center
If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center
profile, as described in Apple's privacy policy.

## Notifications
Optional local reminders are scheduled on your device. They contain no personal data and are never sent to a server.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Contact
Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/khimchuk00/hamster-ages/issues
